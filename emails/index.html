Gap Factory emails

Six Gap Factory emails I built in Salesforce Marketing Cloud in April–June 2023, saved as they went out: promos, merch sends, and earner and non-earner versions of the same day’s GapCash send.

Where
Gap Inc, as Email Developer
Brand
Gap Factory (US)
My role
HTML, AMPscript, versions and QA
Tools
Salesforce Marketing Cloud, AMPscript, Litmus

Subject lines and preheaders are the real ones from each email. Links are turned off. Names and customer data are test data.

In the inbox

What customers saw first

Each send pairs a short, punchy subject line with a preheader that adds the second offer. The June 10 morning send went out in earner and non-earner versions.
